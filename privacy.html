Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

About this application

Obsidian Rclone is a personal configuration of Rclone, used to synchronize Obsidian Markdown vaults between a local Ubuntu computer and Google Drive.

Google Drive Data

The application requests Google Drive access to read, create, modify and synchronize files belonging to the authorized Google account.

Data Storage

Files are stored locally on the user's computer and in the user's Google Drive account.

No separate application server is operated to collect or store personal files.

Data Sharing

The application owner does not sell or share Google Drive data with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Files remain in the user's local storage and Google Drive until deleted by the user or through the configured synchronization process.

Access Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application owner through the GitHub repository.
